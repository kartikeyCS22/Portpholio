About me

I'm a Computer Science graduate and Associate Software Engineer at Accenture. I work where databases meet applications: Oracle SQL, PL/SQL and APEX at the core, React and Node.js on the surface, and generative AI on top.

More recently I've been building with generative AI: LLM applications, RAG, prompt engineering and agents with the Claude API, Claude Agent SDK and MCP. I hold Anthropic and Databricks credentials alongside my Oracle ones, and I'm growing my cloud footprint across Oracle Cloud and Microsoft Azure.
